<objective>
Close **gap 2** from `11-VERIFICATION.md` (`status: partial`): the mode firewall stops at the
storage layer. `RecordRunEndArgs` makes the campaign write structurally unreachable for an
endless run and both stores are proven byte-identical across an endless win — that half is
some of the strongest work in the phase. But `handleRunEnded` (597-645) calls
`evaluatePersonalBest(runScore, previousBestRef.current)` BEFORE it branches on mode, and
`previousBestRef` holds `store.getBestForLevel(levelId)` — a campaign level best. So an
endless run (a) shows the campaign PB as `Best` on the endless Results overlay, (b) fires
`New Record` when an endless score beats an unrelated campaign score, and (c) at 638-640
writes the endless score into `previousBestRef.current`, which `onRetry` and
`remountDevSession` then re-display as the campaign best.

Nothing persists, so SC-3's narrowest reading survives; its plain meaning does not. And
`telemetry.endless` has no reader anywhere in `app/` or `src/` — the record the endless player
just set is the one number never shown to them.

Purpose: make the record the player sees the record the player set.

Output: per-mode watermark refs selected before `evaluatePersonalBest`, a mode-aware
`ResultOverlay`, and the ops record updated to match the contract that now ships.

**Out of scope:** a permanent endless record surface and the "which record is THE record"
display decision are Phase 14 (`ENDLESS-MODE.md` § Limits item 4). This plan ships the
endless Results overlay as the only endless-record reader, and deliberately does not elect a
primary record.
</objective>
