<objective>
Close gap 1 of `11-VERIFICATION.md`: **the record can be wrong on screen.**

`startEndlessRun` no longer publishes `previousBestRef` — 11-11 did that half for real. But
`resultBest` has a SECOND writer: the mount-time `getBestForLevel` preload effect
(`PlayingHost.tsx:460-477`) calls `setResultBest(b)` unconditionally, with `[store, levelId]` deps
and no mode term. The verifier measured the consequence on a MOUNTED endless Results overlay:
`Best ·` flips `4200` (the endless watermark) to `7777` (a campaign level best). A player cannot
chase a number that is not theirs.

It survived two rounds because the WR-04 contract counts `previousBestRef.current =` ASSIGNMENTS
and explicitly whitelists the preload effect as one of "the two campaign-only regions" — so it is
structurally blind to a campaign value being PUBLISHED out of that region into a prop the endless
overlay reads. This plan re-points the contract at `setResultBest` and, because a source contract
is what let this through twice, closes the gap with a BEHAVIOUR test that drives the real host and
reads the real rendered `Best ·` line.

**`N-END-02` moves twice in this plan and that is deliberate, not churn.** Task 1 reverts it to
`[ ]` per the owner's decision of 2026-09-26 — the requirement says endless play cannot alter
campaign bests *as the player sees them*, and a campaign number is provably rendered as the
endless `Best`. Task 3 re-ticks it once the rendered behaviour test is green. The box is unchecked
only while the leak is open; git records both moves and the reason for each.

Purpose: this is the only one of the three gaps that puts a wrong number in front of a player.
Output: one mode-aware publication rule for `resultBest`, a symmetric republication on the endless
exit, a source contract aimed at the causing symbol, a driven render test, a non-vacuous liveness
assertion, and a requirement box that is correct at the end of the round.
