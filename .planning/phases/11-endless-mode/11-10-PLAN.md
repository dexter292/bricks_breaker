defect is placement. The invariant belongs INSIDE `startEndlessRun`, because that function is
what `a new run starts` means — and wiring it at two of five callers left the `__DEV__` `Endless`
button and `toggleDevLevel` discarding live runs with `recordRunEnd` called **zero** times, both
measured against the real host, while `docs/ops/ENDLESS-MODE.md:265-266` asserts in bold that no
such path exists.

— record the in-flight run, then return `modeRef` to `'campaign'`. That is simultaneously gap 1's
second artifact, the minimal discharge of A-02, and the first writer that ever returns `modeRef`
to campaign, which un-breaks the compiled-push gate effect for the rest of the mount. The two
rejected options — disable the control while endless, or defer to Phase 14 — are closed; do not
re-open them.

Purpose: `startEndlessRun` is the function Phase 14 promotes to the production endless entry
point, so an omission here outlives the dev row that exposed it.

mode-aware, and two behaviour tests that press the real controls and assert on what
`recordRunEnd` actually received.
