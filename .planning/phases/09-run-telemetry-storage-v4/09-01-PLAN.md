**Why grid-adjacency, not the cheaper substep heuristic (record of the plan-check decision):**
`largestCascade` feeds a Phase 13 achievement trigger (CONTEXT D-08), and for a trigger,
overcounting (firing an unearned unlock) is the harmful direction, not undercounting — the
opposite of a display-only stat. The substep-level heuristic (treat any BRICK_BREAK co-occurring
with an EXPLOSIVE break in the same substep as one cascade) overcounts whenever a substep
contains one EXPLOSIVE break plus ANY unrelated BRICK_BREAK — plausible during ordinary multiball
play (`MAX_BALLS = 8`), not a rare edge case. Grid-adjacency is exact for every real playable
level (all use the lattice broadphase; `pitchX/pitchY <= 0` only occurs in dense/legacy test
fixtures that ship no explosive bricks). The grouping cost is bounded and cheap: at most 128
collected breaks per call (ring cap), O(breakCount²) pairwise adjacency checks against a tiny
`breakCount` in the overwhelming common case (0 or 1 break per substep), zero heap allocation
(module-level scratch, reused every call, identical in spirit to `_sweepScratch`).
