<objective>
Make the two E2-locked measuring instruments able to see a board that has no file.

`levelStatics` and `runBot` both start with `readLevelFile(id)` from `assets/levels/${id}.json`.
A generated board has no file, and writing one would be non-deterministic, filesystem-bound, and
would break `scripts/assert-level-solvability.mjs` (which sweeps that directory and requires
exactly `level-02.json` to fail). The fix is a behaviour-preserving extract-and-delegate: an
object-taking core plus the existing id-taking wrapper.

Purpose: N-GEN-03 must be asserted against *the* authored-weight definition, not a second one;
N-GEN-02's quality backstop must play the real `stepRun`, not a heuristic.
Output: `tests/helpers/balanceBot.ts` split in place with `tests/balance.curve-e2.test.ts`
unchanged and green, plus the three remaining levelgen test files scaffolded.
</objective>
