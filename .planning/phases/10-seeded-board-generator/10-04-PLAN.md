<objective>
Close the gap between "reachable on paper" and "actually clearable", and write the record a
reader in Phase 11 or Phase 12 will need.

The lint proves reachability, not playability: a board with one hp3 brick tucked behind a steel
rib is reachable and tedious. `runBot` plays the real `stepRun` pipeline with a perfect paddle
at roughly 20 ms per board, so a stratified sample is cheap and catches a *structural* miss — a
brick that is reachable on paper but practically unhittable. It is a measuring instrument that
never misses, so a win is a necessary, not sufficient, condition for a human-playable board;
that limitation belongs in the ops record, not hidden.

Purpose: the SC-2 quality backstop, plus the durable record of the proof and its limits.
Output: `tests/levelgen.winnability.test.ts` live, `docs/ops/BOARD-GENERATOR.md` written.
</objective>
