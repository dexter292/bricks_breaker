<objective>
Close the read half of the daily record: bound it, validate every stored date key, and prove that
a corrupt daily record degrades daily alone. Then pin N-DAILY-03's clock cases against a real
store round trip rather than against a mock.

Purpose: the write bound is what D-15 asks for; the read bound is what makes it hold against a
blob written by an older build or edited on a rooted device. And a firewall that only checks the
write path says nothing about what happens when the blob comes back malformed — the UI-SPEC's
accepted read-failure cost only holds if "unreadable" actually degrades to "no result" rather
than to a rendered 4 000-character string.

Output: `sanitizeDailyRecord` wired into the existing sanitizer chain, its cases in the shipped v4
parser spec, and a new clock-policy spec.
</objective>
