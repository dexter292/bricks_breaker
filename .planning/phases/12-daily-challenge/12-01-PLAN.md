**1. `src/services/daily/dateKey.ts` (new).** Export `localDateKey(nowMs: number): string`
returning the local calendar date as `YYYY-MM-DD`, built from `getFullYear()`,
`getMonth() + 1`, `getDate()` and `padStart`. Export
`DAILY_DIFFICULTY = 10 as const` with the D-11 reasoning stated in a doc comment: on the
generator's published table (docs/ops/BOARD-GENERATOR.md:171-193) `d = 10` gives 12 rows, 72
bricks, 116 authored HP, 3 explosives and 3 steel pairs per half — between `level-01`'s 32
bricks and `level-03`'s 94-brick showpiece, at a ~2-minute median clear time, and exactly the
difficulty endless reaches at wave 11.

The module header copies `src/services/endless/ramp.ts:1-48`'s four-section structure: what is
contract, what is borrowed, why validation lives in this body, and the banned-primitive rule.
Its banned-primitive rule is the ECMA-402 namespace, every locale-formatting `Date` method and
the UTC-serialising method — all three forbidden in this module. The evidence is executed, not
asserted: 12-RESEARCH § Finding 2 shows one instant giving five different keys across five
device locales (Buddhist, Persian, Islamic and two Gregorian forms), and § Finding 3(e) shows
the UTC serialiser is a calendar day wrong for a third of every day in Santiago. Adding a fixed
day in milliseconds instead of using the constructor is banned for the reason in § Finding 3(c):
it skips 2026-09-06 outright in Santiago and repeats 2026-11-01 in Havana. That last clause is
forward-stated on purpose: no function in this module steps a day yet, and the one that does —
`nextLocalMidnightMs` — arrives in plan 12-02, written test-first beside the two real 2026 DST
days that are the only thing able to falsify it. The rule is written here because it is a
property of the module, and plan 12-02 inherits it together with this task's comment-stripped
grep gate over `src/services/daily/*.ts`.
