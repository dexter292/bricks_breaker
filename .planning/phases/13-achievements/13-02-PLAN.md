**Every threshold's JSDoc copies `DAILY_DIFFICULTY`'s model** and must contain, in this order:
what the number is, the reasoning that produced it, the two anchors it sits between where two
exist, and — mandatory, in these terms — that it is a JUDGEMENT and not a measurement, because no
human has played this game and there is therefore no distribution to derive from. Where an anchor
exists in a published document, cite it by file; where none exists, write that no anchor exists
rather than inventing a plausible-sounding one. Each JSDoc closes by naming what a re-tune would
cost: nothing in code, because the catalog is data — which is SC-1, restated where it is actually
load-bearing.

Reasoning to state, per entry (expand each into the `DAILY_DIFFICULTY` shape; these are the
arguments, not the final prose):

- **2 `25x Combo`** — `bestComboEver` counts brick hits without paddle contact (`telemetry.ts`
  module header, D-06). Anchored: `level-03` is the 94-brick showpiece and the generator's
  published table puts `d = 10` at 72 bricks, so 25 is roughly a third of a mid-scale board
  cleared in one unbroken sequence. Judgement, not measurement.
- **3 `60 Rally`** — `longestRallyEver` counts paddle hits without losing a life (D-10). Anchored
  against nothing published; 60 is chosen as "about a minute of unbroken play" at a plausible
  paddle-contact cadence, and that cadence is itself unmeasured. Say so.
- **4 `12 Cascade`** — `largestCascadeEver` is a single chain reaction. Anchored: explosive bricks
  exist (`docs/ops/EXPLOSIVE-BRICKS.md`) and the generator places three per half at `d = 10`, so
  12 is a chain a well-placed shot into a dense region could plausibly reach and a stray one could
  not. Judgement.
- **5 `50 Runs`** — cumulative across every mode, unfailable. 50 is a returning player rather than
  a curious one. No anchor exists; say so.
- **6 `100 Pickups`** — the sum of `pickupMultiball`, `pickupExpand`, `pickupExtraLife`,
  `pickupSlow` and `pickupFireball`. Summed rather than five separate achievements because five
  near-identical entries is the tiering D-09 rejected wearing a different hat. 100 across five
  kinds is roughly 20 of each. No anchor; say so.
- **7 `Flawless Clear`** — reads `byMode.campaign` and is DELIBERATELY conservative: the aggregate
  is per level ACROSS runs, so `runsWon >= 1 && livesLost === 0` means the player has won that
  level and has never lost a life on it, which is strictly stronger than "cleared it once without
  losing a life". State that this under-reports on purpose and that under-reporting is this tree's
  chosen direction everywhere — a lifetime best invented out of an aggregate is a worse failure
  than one that arrives late. A player who once lost a life on `level-01` and later plays it
  perfectly will not get this on `level-01`; they will get it on the next level they never fumble.
- **8 `25 Clears`** — `runsWon` summed over `byMode.campaign`. Cumulative, campaign-only, and the
  companion to 7: one rewards precision on a level, the other persistence across the campaign.
- **9 `Wave 10`** — `endless.bestWave`. **Anchored, and this is the one threshold in the catalog
  with a real anchor**: `DAILY_DIFFICULTY = 10` is documented at `src/services/daily/dateKey.ts`
  as exactly the difficulty endless reaches at wave 11, and the generator's published table
  (`docs/ops/BOARD-GENERATOR.md`) puts `d = 10` between `level-01`'s 32 bricks and `level-03`'s
  94. Wave 10 is therefore "as deep as the daily board is hard", which is a real relationship and
  not a round number. Still a judgement about whether that depth is worth an achievement.
- **10 `20 Endless`** — `runsPlayed` summed over `byMode.endless` values. Cumulative, endless-only.
  No anchor; say so.
- **11 `Perfect Daily`** — reads `byMode.daily`, symmetric with 7 and conservative for the same
  reason. Note the additional daily-specific fact: `byMode.daily` is keyed by a single constant
  (D-15 of phase 12), so unlike campaign this aggregate spans every date, which makes it a claim
  about the player's whole daily history and therefore harder than 7 rather than easier. Say so —
  a reader will otherwise assume the two are equivalent.
- **12 `7 Day Streak`** — `daily.longestStreak`. **This entry is where D-16 binds and it must say
  so:** the value is read AS STORED and inherits T-12-06's accepted tamper model — both
  `11-SECURITY.md` and `12-SECURITY.md` close local record tampering as an accepted risk, not a
  defect, because there is no server, no leaderboard and no asset being protected. State the
  rejected alternative in the same breath, because it looks like the safer choice and is not:
  deriving the streak from the trimmed history instead would make a streak longer than the
  400-entry window read as the window length, which is precisely the failure phase 12's D-16 was
  re-opened mid-phase to eliminate. Seven days is a week; no anchor exists for whether a week is
  the right week.
