declared as data → a pure predicate over a structurally-compatible telemetry snapshot → evaluated
once inside `recordRunEnd`, outside every mode gate → the union persisted in the v4 blob with a

of three places if a site is missed (D-23); and the two hand-mirrored stores must agree. A tracer
puts all four under one runnable check before any horizontal build-out is committed.

the sixteen files are one tracer because the two compiler-forced sets below make them one, and
splitting means the first half ships a widened `recordRunEnd` whose delta nothing yet consumes.
Take the cut only if the context budget actually forces it, and say so in the summary.
