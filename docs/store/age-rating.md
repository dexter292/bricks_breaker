# Age Rating — Pulse Paddle

| Store / system | Target |

## Content rationale

Pulse Paddle is a classic paddle-and-ball arcade game:

- **No realistic or cartoon violence** against people or animals — bricks are abstract neon blocks
- **No blood, gore, or weapons** themed as real-world violence
- **No gambling, simulated gambling, or loot boxes**
- **No user-generated content, chat, or social features**
- **No sexual content, horror, or substance references**
- **No ads or IAP** in the MVP that would introduce ad-network content ratings

Gameplay is skill-based scoring and clearing brick layouts. Sound effects are short arcade cues; there is no spoken dialogue.

## Confirmation

These answers match the shipped MVP content (levels, SFX, art, and shell UI). Revisit this document if future builds add UGC, competitive chat, or mature themes.
